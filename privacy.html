Beyond the Horizon — Privacy Policy

Luna Corporation · Azerbaijan · Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Azərbaycan dilində

This policy explains what the mobile game Beyond the Horizon ("the game"), published by Luna Corporation ("we", "us"), collects, why, who receives it and what choices you have. The game is not directed at children under 13.

1. Data we process

DataWhyWho processes it
Game progress (systems reached, ships, upgrades, in-game resources, settings)To save your game on the device and keep a backup copy so it survives a reinstall or a new phoneYour device; Unity Technologies (Unity Cloud Save)

Account identifier and e-mail address — only if you choose "Sign in" (Google or Unity account)So your progress follows your account to another deviceUnity Technologies (Unity Player Accounts); Google, if you sign in with Google

Crash and diagnostics dataTo fix errorsUnity Technologies; Google Play Console

We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone. We do not sell personal data.

2. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Saving and backing up your progress and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you (the game). Personalised advertising: your consent, asked on the first screen and changeable at any time in Settings → Privacy. Fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest.

3. Advertising

Ads are never forced: a rewarded ad plays only when you tap an offer (second chance, emergency fuel and air, double loot). If you do not consent, or say you are under 13, ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not used for tracking. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. If a player tells us they are under 13, ads are never personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Storage, security and retention

Data sent to Unity and Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The cloud backup is kept while your player account exists; game data on the device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Unity and Google may store data on servers outside your country under their own safeguards.

6. Your rights and deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, including the cloud backup and your player account, by writing to the address below; please include the player ID shown in Settings under the cloud save line if you can. We delete the cloud backup and the player account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. You can withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy at any time.

7. Third parties

  • Unity Technologies (Cloud Save, Authentication, Player Accounts, LevelPlay, Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play (purchases, distribution): policies.google.com/privacy

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, show the consent screen again in the game.
