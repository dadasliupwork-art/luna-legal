Landfall Protocol — Privacy Policy

Luna Corporation · Azerbaijan · Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Azərbaycan dilində

This policy explains what the mobile game Landfall Protocol ("the game"), published by Luna Corporation ("we", "us"), collects, why, who receives it and what choices you have. The game is not directed at children under 13. To delete your account and data, see section 7 or the account deletion page.

2. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Saving and backing up your progress and delivering purchases: performance of our contract with you (the game). Personalised advertising: your consent, asked on the first screen and changeable at any time in Settings → Privacy. Diagnostics, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in a working and safe game.

3. Advertising

Ads are never forced: a rewarded ad plays only when you tap an offer (second chance, emergency fuel and air, double loot). The game contains these advertising SDKs:

  • Unity LevelPlay — the ironSource mediation SDK (Unity Technologies / ironSource Mobile Ltd.), which chooses which network shows an ad, together with ironSource Ads;
  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), connected through LevelPlay.

To serve, measure and protect ads they collect and share among themselves and with advertisers: the advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, your interaction with the ad (views and taps inside the ad, not your gameplay) and ad diagnostics. They process this under their own privacy policy: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy (covers Unity Ads, Unity LevelPlay and ironSource) and Unity Privacy Policy.

Your choice is passed to them: if you do not consent, or say you are under 13, ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not used for tracking. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy, and reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).

4. Unity Developer Data

The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Player Accounts, Cloud Save, In-App Purchasing). For our Unity project we have enabled Unity's Developer Data collection at the Essential level and allowed its use for technical support.

  • What: technical data from the game and the Unity services — crash logs, performance and stability telemetry, device model and OS version, engine and configuration data, and the identifiers the services need (player ID, installation ID).
  • Why: so Unity can run, secure and improve the services the game uses, keep the engine stable on your device, and help us when we open a support request.
  • Controller status: for these purposes Unity Technologies receives the data as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy, not only on our behalf.
  • More information and your rights towards Unity: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

5. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. If a player tells us they are under 13, ads are never personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Storage, security and retention

Data sent to Unity and Google travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). The cloud backup is kept while your player account exists; game data on the device is deleted when you uninstall the game. Unity and Google may store data on servers outside your country under their own safeguards.

7. Your rights and deleting your account

In the game: open Settings → Delete my account and cloud data and confirm. The game deletes your cloud backup and your player account (player ID and any linked Google or Unity sign-in) right away and signs you out; the cloud stays off on that phone until you sign in again. It then asks whether to erase the progress on the phone as well.

Without the game (for example if it is no longer installed): write to lunacorp.app@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Landfall Protocol account" and, if you can, the player ID shown in Settings under the cloud save line. We delete the cloud backup and the player account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is kept: purchases are recorded by Google Play, not by us; they stay with your Google account and come back with Restore purchases. Data held by the ad partners and by Unity as an independent controller (sections 3 and 4) is handled under their own policies; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Step-by-step instructions: delete-account.html.

You can also ask us to access or correct your data, and withdraw ad consent in Settings → Privacy at any time.
