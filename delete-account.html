Landfall Protocol — Delete your account and data

Luna Corporation (developer of Landfall Protocol on Google Play) · Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Azərbaycan dilində

This page explains how to delete your Landfall Protocol player account and the data linked to it. You can do it in the game or, if the game is no longer installed, by e-mail.

Option 1 — in the game (immediate)

  1. Open Landfall Protocol and tap Settings (in the main menu or the pause menu).
  2. Tap Delete my account and cloud data.
  3. Confirm. The cloud backup and the player account are deleted right away and the game signs you out.
  4. The game then asks whether to erase the progress on this phone too. Choose Yes to start from the beginning, or No to keep playing offline with the progress on the phone.

Option 2 — without the game (within 30 days)

  1. Send an e-mail to lunacorp.app@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Landfall Protocol account.
  2. If you can, add your player ID (shown in the game in Settings, under the cloud save line) or the e-mail address of the Google or Unity account you signed in with.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • The cloud backup of your game progress (Unity Cloud Save).
  • Your player account: the player ID and any linked Google or Unity sign-in (Unity Authentication / Player Accounts).
  • If you choose so in the game: the progress saved on the phone. Uninstalling the game also removes it.

What is kept

  • Purchases: Google Play keeps the purchase records with your Google account (we do not hold them). They come back with Settings → Restore purchases.
  • Advertising data held by Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads, and technical data Unity holds as an independent controller, under their own retention rules (Unity's player privacy policy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads).
  • Your settings on the phone (language, sound, consent) until you uninstall the game.

More details: Privacy Policy.

Hesabı və məlumatları silmək

Oyunda (dərhal): Landfall Protocol → Ayarlar → Hesabımı və bulud məlumatlarımı sil → təsdiqlə. Bulud nüsxəsi və oyunçu hesabı dərhal silinir, oyun hesabdan çıxır; sonra telefondakı irəliləyişi də silmək istəyib-istəmədiyini soruşur.

Oyunsuz (30 gün ərzində): lunacorp.app@gmail.com ünvanına "Delete my Landfall Protocol account" mövzusu ilə yaz, mümkünsə Ayarlardakı oyunçu ID-sini və ya daxil olduğun hesabın e-poçtunu əlavə et. Silib e-poçtla bildiririk.

Silinir: bulud nüsxəsi, oyunçu ID-si və bağlı Google/Unity girişi; istəsən telefondakı irəliləyiş. Qalır: Google Play-in alış qeydləri («Alışları bərpa et» ilə qayıdır), reklam tərəfdaşlarının və Unity-nin öz siyasəti ilə saxladığı məlumat, telefondakı ayarlar (oyun silinənə qədər).
